Teach Donut her name

She is going to hear you say "Hey Donut" a few hundred times. Twenty minutes, and she stops mishearing you.

  • Put the phone down where you normally stand or sit. Do not hold it to your mouth.
  • It records in bursts. When it says SPEAK, say “Hey Donut” once, normally.
  • It keeps going on its own. Pause any time. Nothing is lost if you stop.
  • Do the rooms you actually use her in. More rooms is better.
